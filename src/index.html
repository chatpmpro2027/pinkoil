    The 5-minute ritual

    Your new favorite self-care moment.

    Use 2–3 times a week. Consistency is the secret. Most people see the difference by week eight.

    3:00

    Scalp massage timer

    Three minutes of gentle circles wakes up the scalp.

    1. 01

      Part

      Section dry or damp hair into 1-inch parts to expose the scalp.

    2. 02

      Drop

      Apply 3–5 drops along each part with the dropper, about one full pipette in total.

    3. 03

      Massage

      Use your fingertips in slow circles for three minutes. Smooth any extra through your ends.

    4. 04

      Rest & rinse

      Leave it in for at least an hour, or overnight, then shampoo as usual.

    Most people notice a healthier-feeling scalp and shinier hair within the first two weeks, less shedding around week four, and visibly fuller-looking hair from weeks eight to twelve with consistent use 2–3 times a week.

    Is it safe for color-treated, keratin-treated or curly hair?

    Yes. Pink Oil is free from silicones, sulfates, parabens, mineral oil and synthetic fragrance, and suits all hair types and textures, including color-treated, keratin-treated and chemically processed hair.

    Will it make my hair greasy?

    Leave it on for at least an hour, or overnight, and shampoo as usual. Hair is left soft and glossy, not greasy. For fine hair, focus on the scalp and use 3 drops per part.

    Can I use it while pregnant or breastfeeding?

    Pink Oil contains rosemary and peppermint essential oils. Please check with your doctor before use during pregnancy or breastfeeding, and patch-test first if you have sensitive skin.
